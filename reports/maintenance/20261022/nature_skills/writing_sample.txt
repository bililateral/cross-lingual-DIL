English draft
In the fixed-seed A→B development experiment, H_pos achieved higher final mean average precision (MAP) than S on the old domain A (0.5261 versus 0.4941; difference, +0.0319; conditional 95% confidence interval, 0.0154 to 0.0485). On the new domain B, MAP was slightly lower (0.4997 versus 0.5059; difference, −0.0062; conditional 95% confidence interval, −0.0195 to 0.0072). Thus, this comparison supported an old-domain ranking gain under the evaluated conditions, but did not establish improvement on both domains. The intervals were conditional on the fitted models and did not quantify variation across training seeds or model selection. This secondary comparison did not replace the study's unsuccessful primary S_pos-versus-S criterion.
